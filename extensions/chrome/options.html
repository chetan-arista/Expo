What ARACHNIX Companion may do, and why

  • Active tab: read the page you are on, only when you open ARACHNIX Companion on it, for the on-page check.
  • Scripting: run that check inside the page. It reads the page, changes nothing and sends nothing.
  • Storage: keep these settings on this computer.
  • Your ARACHNIX address (asked when you save): talk to your ARACHNIX server, and nothing else.
  • Cookies and that site's address (asked each time you click "Use my session in ARACHNIX"): read the cookies your browser would send to that one site, send them to your ARACHNIX server once, then give the access back. Cookie values are never shown or kept by the extension.
